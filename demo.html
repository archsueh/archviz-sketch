Hand-drawn Chart Styles

1. Xiaohei Style (小黑怪诞)

Pure white background, black line art, red accents

Animation: bars growing from bottom with hand-drawn wobble effect

2. Available Styles

xiaohei

小黑怪诞风格
Pure white background
Black line art
Red/orange/blue accents

minimal-line

极简线条风格
Clean thin lines
Color accents
Strong negative space

process-draft

过程稿风格
Paper texture
Construction lines
Imperfect hand-drawn feel

3. Usage

python3 scripts/render_hand_drawn_chart.py \
  --spec examples/hand-drawn-chart-spec.json \
  --outdir ./output \
  --basename my-chart \
  --style xiaohei \
  --frames 30 \
  --fps 15
