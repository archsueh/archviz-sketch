Carl Liu Style Concept Sketch

1. Concept Device Sketch

Industrial design concept sketch with selective color and handwritten annotations

Animation: device reveal with construction lines and annotations

2. Style Features (Inspired by Carl Liu)

Bold Ink Outlines

Heavy black lines for main forms, thin lines for construction guides

Selective Color

Color only on key elements (purple, teal, orange-red)

Handwritten Annotations

Hand-drawn text with arrows pointing to components

Component Breakdown

Exploded view showing internal parts

Construction Lines

Light guidelines showing structure and alignment

Unfinished Feel

Sketch-like quality with wobble and imperfections

3. All Sketch Styles

Xiaohei (小黑怪诞)

Pure white background, black line art, red accents

Carl Liu (概念设计)

Industrial design, selective color, annotations

4. Usage

# Xiaohei style
python3 scripts/render_hand_drawn_chart.py \
  --spec data.json \
  --style xiaohei

# Carl Liu style
python3 scripts/render_concept_sketch.py \
  --spec concept-spec.json
